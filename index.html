Questions about the 인성검사

What is a 인성검사?

It's the personality test most large Korean companies give applicants after document screening. You rate hundreds of short statements about yourself; there are no right answers, but companies check consistency, honesty and fit.

How long is the real test?

Usually 200–400+ statements in 40–60 minutes, about 7–10 seconds each. The full-length simulation here matches that.

Is it only in Korean?

Most tests are in Korean. Practise with translations first, then switch to exam mode to build reading speed.

Is this the real test?

No. Every question here is an original practice item based on common formats, not any company's actual test.
